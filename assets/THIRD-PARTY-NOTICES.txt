BolBeats includes or is built on:

- Feishin (GPL-3.0), the music player BolBeats is a modified version of.
  Source: https://github.com/jeffvli/feishin
  BolBeats' changes are marked "BolBeats:" in the source and listed in the BolBeats repository.

- TagLib (LGPL-2.1 or MPL-1.1), through taglib-wasm (MIT), reads song tags and covers for the music library and the
  tag editor. Source: https://github.com/taglib/taglib and https://github.com/CharlesWiltgen/taglib-wasm

- Earlier versions (1.0 to 1.5) bundled Navidrome (GPL-3.0) as the music library. BolBeats' own library replaced it and
  contains none of its code; on the first launch it reads Navidrome's database once to keep your ids, plays and playlists.

- Electron (MIT) and Chromium (various open-source licences; see LICENSES.chromium.html next to this file's folder).

- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque, Instrument Sans and Inter (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

Online lookups (only when you use them): iTunes Search API (Apple), MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive. BolBeats
sends them song and artist names only. Store previews play only next to a link to the store, as Apple asks.
